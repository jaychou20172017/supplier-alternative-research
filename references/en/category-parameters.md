# Category parameter prompts

[中文](../category-parameters.md)

Select relevant parameters from the actual material/application; these prompts are not mandatory requirements. Use uploaded/official terminology, units and test conditions; unknown fields stay blank.

Electronics: function/pins, package/footprint, voltages/currents, tolerance, temperature, timing/interface, power, lifetime, EMC, RoHS/REACH and obsolescence; same package does not prove pin compatibility.
Motion: motor/drive/encoder type, rated/peak torque/speed, power, inertia, resolution, control, supply/brake, shaft/flange/mounting, cooling, protection and safety; check mechanical and control compatibility.
Optics: wavelength/spectrum, transmission/reflection, focal length/NA, resolution/distortion, working distance/aperture, coating, dimensions, environmental stability, laser threshold or sensor interface; compare measurement conditions.
Fluids: medium/material compatibility, flow/pressure/drop, accuracy/response/leakage, ports/mounting, wetted materials, cleanliness/sterilization/biocompatibility, temperature and life.
Power/batteries: input/output, power/efficiency/ripple/transients/protection/thermal/interface; chemistry, capacity/energy, C-rate, cycle life, BMS, transport and safety certification. Across categories ask about real use, target market, customer standards, volume and PCN/EOL continuity; do not silently make absent conditions hard gates.
