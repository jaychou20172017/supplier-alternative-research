# Candidate admission and ranking

[中文](../decision-rules.md)

A model-level candidate requires verified manufacturer identity and official evidence identifying the complete model/suffix with function and principal interface/mounting characteristics. A series source must explicitly map to that model. Count each manufacturer once. Series-only, reseller-only or identity/model gaps are leads, not counted candidates; documented hard-requirement failures are exclusions. With no confirmed hard conditions, label a provisional similarity screen and its assumptions.

For every key parameter record match/conflict/missing evidence/different conditions, both values, units, tolerances and sources. Do not compare different test conditions as equal. Missing critical evidence cannot support an easy rating.

Rank assessable candidates easy → medium → difficult; pending validation is separate at the end. Easy means official functional/interface/mounting evidence reveals no known wiring/mechanical/control changes, not proven interchangeability. Medium means limited wiring/settings/tolerance work; difficult means mechanical/circuit/control/certification redesign or substantially more validation. Missing/conflicting model evidence means pending. Within a band compare technical/change workload, same-entity quality/supply evidence, then public price/lead time. No aggregate score may hide hard failures.

Corporate facts retain legal entity, year, metric basis, source type and verification status. Registered/paid-in capital, insured workers/total employees, group factories/model production facilities are distinct. Certificates need issuer, number, covered entity/factory and validity; a claim alone is unverified.
