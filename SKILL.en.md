# Alternative Manufacturer Research — English instructions
[中文](SKILL.md)

This is the English companion to the existing SKILL.md, not a separate skill installation. Read the [reference index](references/en/index.md) and applicable references.

## Preparation and technical baseline
Read uploaded specifications, drawings, BOM, certifications and existing-supplier material; record file, version and date. Identify product category, application, current model and supplier. Ask one grouped question when missing operating conditions, hard technical/regulatory requirements, allowed wiring/mechanical changes or target market could alter exclusion/ranking. If the user defers, continue a clearly labeled provisional similarity screen. Do not turn every original-product parameter into a mandatory condition. If product identity/use cannot be determined, ask the minimum critical questions first.

Use the host's actual web/document capabilities. Corporate-platform pages/screenshots/exports may be used with attribution and access limits; never assume shared paid accounts. Without Internet access, do not fabricate a verified manufacturer shortlist. Honor a partial requested scope rather than forcing the full workflow.

Extract function, interfaces, dimensions/mounting, performance, operating conditions, lifetime/reliability, regulatory and quality requirements. Preserve values, units, tolerances, test conditions, source/page, mandatory/preference/unknown classification and rated/limit/typical/guaranteed distinction. Preserve conflicts and gaps in the per-parameter evidence ledger.

## Existing supplier and alternative models
Distinguish brand, manufacturer, selling entity, parent and production entity. Collect only attributable facts: legal identity, website, locations, formation, capital where applicable, workforce/year/basis, scope, certifications, factory area, patents, equipment, capacity and substantiated industry position. Keep source/date/entity and verification state per fact. Platform data is third-party aggregation; insured headcount is not total employees. Validate registrations, certificates and patents through primary sources when possible. Self-disclosure is not independent verification; missing data is not zero.

Search Chinese/English functions, key specifications, original model, standards and manufacturer catalogs. Aim for 5–8 distinct model-level verified manufacturers, with a domestic majority when sufficient qualified options exist; never pad the count. Distributors/traders do not count as manufacturers. Classify by headquarters/control separately from manufacturing location.

Each counted manufacturer requires its official homepage and a model-specific official page/catalog/datasheet showing the complete part number and suffix. A series page counts only with an explicit model mapping. Compare parameters under equivalent conditions. Hard-condition conflicts are excluded; missing/conflicting evidence remains pending. Rank by mandatory technical/compliance conditions, validation/change workload, quality/supply evidence, then public price/lead time. Do not invent unavailable prices or call unvalidated candidates drop-in replacements. Check sale/discontinuation, PCN/EOL and continuity signals with stated gaps.

## Segment and delivery
Define product, application and geography. Include an upstream materials/components → manufacturers → downstream applications diagram, distinguishing sourced facts from structural inference. Market figures need year, currency/unit, region, definition and forecast source; adjacent-market data is context, not the target segment's size.

Deliver the default Chinese `.docx` with editable candidate ranking, technical and supplier comparison tables, clickable official links, exclusions/pending items, segment overview and sources. Full tasks also deliver UTF-8 `关键参数证据.csv`; comparison CSVs are optional. Generate all outputs from the same checked records, not independent transcription. Review every rendered Word page for fonts, charts, wrapping, pagination and links, and reconcile models, tiers, ordering, values and evidence. If .docx cannot be generated, state the obstacle and provide full convertible text/tables; do not call Markdown a Word deliverable.

Before delivery check actual manufacturer identities, exact links/models, direct parameter evidence, corporate dates/bases, hard-condition ranking and consistent market definitions. Record source type separately from verification status. This is public-information research, not sample validation, supplier admission or engineering/quality approval. Do not automatically contact suppliers, inquire prices or order.
